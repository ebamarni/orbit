About Kurdistan

Kurdistan is a region known for its rich culture, stunning mountains, warm hospitality, and ancient history. It stretches across parts of Iraq, Turkey, Iran, and Syria.

Top Destinations

Erbil

The capital city, home to the ancient Erbil Citadel — one of the oldest continuously inhabited sites in the world.

Duhok

Famous for its beautiful mountains, lakes, and peaceful landscapes.

Sulaymaniyah

A cultural center known for art, education, and vibrant city life.

Culture & Heritage

Kurdish culture is rich in music, dance, traditional clothing, and festivals such as Newroz. The region is also known for its delicious cuisine and strong community values.
